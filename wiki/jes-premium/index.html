Frequently asked questions

What is JES?

Just an Earth Server is a 1:300 scale Minecraft Earth server, online since 2020, with over 300K unique players.

What is Premium?

Premium is an all-inclusive paid rank that supports the long-term running of the server and gives subscribers handy features and cosmetics. It helps keep JES free to play for everyone.

How are payments secured?

All payments are processed by PayNow, which handles the entire transaction. JES never sees, stores or has access to your card details.

How long does it last?

Choose 1, 3, 6 or 12 months. It renews until you cancel, and longer plans cost less per month.

Can I gift Premium?

Yes, with the Premium Egg. It is an item you can trade or gift, and whoever activates it gets one month of Premium.

What happens to the double ender chest if Premium runs out?

The ender chest goes back to normal size.

How do I get help from a human?
