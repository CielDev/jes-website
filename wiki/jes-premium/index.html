Frequently asked questions

What is Premium?

A paid, all-inclusive rank that helps keep JES online and free to play for everyone.

How long does it last?

Choose 1, 3, 6 or 12 months. It renews until you cancel, and longer plans cost less per month.

Can I gift it?

Yes, with the Premium Egg. It's an item you can trade or gift, and it gives one month of Premium when activated.

What happens when Premium runs out?

Your perks stop, and your ender chest goes back to normal size.
