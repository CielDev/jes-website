JES Wiki

Welcome

Just an Earth Server (JES) is a Minecraft Earth server at 1:300 scale. Pick a spot on the map, build a town, grow it into a nation and trade with everyone else. The world never resets, and it has been running since 2020.

How to join

Add jes.earth to your server list. Bedrock players also need port 19132. Full steps are on the How to Join page.

Town guide

Towns run on the Towny plugin. A town is a group of players sharing claimed land, and it's the best way to protect your builds.

  • /town new <name> creates a town where you stand.
  • /town add <player> invites someone.
  • /town join <name> joins an open town.
  • /town spawn takes you home.
  • /town leave leaves your town.

Not ready to found one? Find a town near you on the map and ask to join. Selling houses and apartments to members is a great way to earn.

Claiming land

Land is claimed in chunks, the 16x16 squares of the world. Turn on Chunks in the map panel to see them.

  • /town claim claims the chunk you're standing in.
  • /town unclaim gives it back.
  • /plot forsale <price> puts a plot up for sale.
  • /plot claim buys the plot you're on.

Claimed land is protected from anyone outside your town.

Nation guide

When towns team up, they form a nation. Nations are where the politics starts: alliances, treaties and years of diplomacy.

  • /nation new <name> founds a nation with your town as capital.
  • /nation add <town> invites a town.
  • /nation ally add <nation> adds an ally.
  • /nation leave takes your town out.

Map guide

The live map shows the whole world as a globe that unrolls into a flat map when you zoom in.

  • Player heads show who is online and where. Click one to fly to them.
  • Colored areas are claims. Click one for town details.
  • The Map panel toggles players, claims, landmarks, chunks and the flat view.
  • Use the online counter to open the full player list.

Gold

Gold ingots are the server currency. There's no fake money to grind. Prices are set by players, not the server.

You earn gold by mining, voting and selling goods in player shops.

Shops

Everything on the market is player-run, so what something costs depends on who is selling it. Set up a shop in your town, price your goods fairly and undercut your rivals.

mcMMO

mcMMO adds skills that level up as you play: mine to get better at mining, fight to get better at fighting. Higher levels unlock abilities.

  • /mcstats shows your skills.
  • /mctop shows the top players.

Ranks and store

Ranks are a free thank you for supporting the server. They're based on your total spend in the store, from Seed up to Astral. Open "How does the rank system work?" there for the full list.
