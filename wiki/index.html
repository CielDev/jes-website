JES Wiki

Welcome

Just an Earth Server (JES) is a Minecraft Earth server at 1:300 scale. Pick a spot on the map, build a town, grow it into a nation and trade with everyone else. The world never resets, and it has been running since 2020.

How to join

Add jes.earth to your server list. Bedrock players also need port 19132. Full steps are on the How to Join page.

Town guide

Towns run on the Towny plugin. A town is a group of players sharing claimed land, and it's the best way to protect your builds.

  • /town new <name> creates a town where you stand.
  • /town add <player> invites someone.
  • /town join <name> joins an open town.
  • /town spawn takes you home.
  • /town leave leaves your town.

Not ready to found one? Find a town near you on the map and ask to join. Selling houses and apartments to members is a great way to earn.

Claiming land

Land is claimed in chunks, the 16x16 squares of the world. Turn on Chunks in the map panel to see them.

  • /town claim claims the chunk you're standing in.
  • /town unclaim gives it back.
  • /plot forsale <price> puts a plot up for sale.
  • /plot claim buys the plot you're on.

Claimed land is protected from anyone outside your town.

Nation guide

When towns team up, they form a nation. Nations are where the politics starts: alliances, treaties and years of diplomacy.

  • /nation new <name> founds a nation with your town as capital.
  • /nation add <town> invites a town.
  • /nation ally add <nation> adds an ally.
  • /nation leave takes your town out.

Map guide

The live map shows the whole world as a globe that unrolls into a flat map when you zoom in.

  • Player heads show who is online and where. Click one to fly to them.
  • Colored areas are claims. Click one for town details.
  • The Map panel toggles players, claims, landmarks, chunks and the flat view.
  • Use the online counter to open the full player list.

Teleporting

There's no player to player teleporting. You can reach your town, your nation, allied capitals and your bed.

  • /t spawn or /t spawn <town> goes to a town spawn.
  • /t warp <warp> or /t warp <town> <warp> goes to a town warp.
  • /n spawn goes to your nation capital, the star on the map.
  • /n spawn <nation> goes to an allied capital.
  • /res spawn or /home goes to your bed.
  • /rtp sends new players to a random spot.
  • /rtp to region <region> sends new players to a random spot in a region, such as Africa.

New towns can't teleport to other nations until the owner joins a nearby nation with /n join <nation>.

Chat

  • /global is the default channel. Keep it in English.
  • /local only reaches nearby players.
  • /tradechat is for buying and selling.
  • /tc is town chat and /nc is nation chat.
  • /ch list shows your channels. /ch join and /ch leave change them.
  • /msg <player> <message> sends a private message and /r replies.
  • /mail send <player> <message> reaches offline players. /mail read and /mail clear manage your inbox.
  • /ignore <player> and /unignore <player> hide or restore someone.

Gold

Gold ingots are the server currency. There's no fake money to grind. Prices are set by players, not the server.

You earn gold by mining, voting and selling goods in player shops.

Shops

Everything on the market is player-run, so what something costs depends on who is selling it. Set up a shop in your town, price your goods fairly and undercut your rivals.

mcMMO

mcMMO adds skills that level up as you play: mine to get better at mining, fight to get better at fighting. Higher levels unlock abilities.

  • /mcstats shows your skills.
  • /mctop shows the top players.

Ranks and store

Ranks are a free thank you for supporting the server. They're based on your total spend in the store, from Seed up to Astral. Open "How does the rank system work?" there for the full list.

Server rules

1. Cheating, exploits and alts

  • 1.1 Cheats: No cheat clients, macros or stolen accounts. Performance, HUD, minimap and cosmetic mods are fine, as is Litematica without a printer.
  • 1.2 Assisting: Knowingly helping or benefiting from a rule break counts as breaking it.
  • 1.3 Exploits: Using bugs without telling staff can mean a permanent ban.
  • 1.4 Alts: One account per player, no sharing. Deliberate alts get banned.
  • 1.5 Disruption: Causing lag on purpose or harming our services can mean a permanent ban.

2. Behavior and chat

  • 2.1 Naming: Nations must match a real current or past nation, region, province or large area, with the capital inside those borders. Town names can't be inappropriate, toxic or offensive, and a real place name goes to the town closest to that place. Usernames, nicknames, skins and titles can't be advertising, explicit or inappropriate.
  • 2.2 Raiding: Raiding and stealing are allowed. Protect your stuff.
  • 2.3 Spam: Don't repeat messages or disrupt chat.
  • 2.4 Behavior: No bullying, hate speech, encouraging self-harm or violence, sexual content or harmful false claims about people. Grooming or inappropriate relationships are strictly banned, on and off the server.
  • 2.5 Impersonation: Don't impersonate players, towns or nations.
  • 2.6 Personal information: Never share someone's personal information without explicit permission, or use it to threaten anyone.
  • 2.7 Links: No malicious, sexual or extremely violent links, or links that help break rules.
  • 2.8 Advertising: Only server-related advertising.
  • 2.9 Real-money trading: No selling in-game items or services for real money, or the reverse.
  • 2.10 Language: Keep global chat in English. Other languages go in private channels.

3. Griefing

  • 3.1 Near claims: Don't damage land in or within 5 chunks of a claim, and don't found towns near others to disrupt them. That's a warning and rollback first, and repeat offenses can mean a permanent ban.
  • 3.2 Wilderness: Changing unclaimed land away from claims is allowed, but anyone can ask for major damage to be rolled back. Don't break railways or ice roads linking two towns.
  • 3.3 Claims: No claim blocking. Towns can't have unclaimed chunks inside their borders, and overclaims can't split a town. Ships are exempt, and ocean outpost arms are fine if unclaimed later.
  • 3.4 Blocks: Taking valuable blocks is allowed, unless they're easy to get.
  • 3.5 Terraforming: Don't make changes visible on the map, like draining lakes or building islands. Vertical terraforming is fine.
  • 3.6 Map art: Allowed if it doesn't interfere with towns or involve major terraforming, and it must follow section 2. Greenland is recommended. Elsewhere, claim it.
