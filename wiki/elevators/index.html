Elevators

Elevators take you between floors instantly. Build one on each floor and you never need another staircase.

What to build

An elevator is one carpet of any color placed on top of one of these blocks:

  • Wool of any color
  • Quartz block
  • Quartz pillar
  • Block of redstone

How to use it

  1. Build an elevator on every floor you want to reach.
  2. Line them up exactly, in the same column.
  3. Stand on the carpet.
  4. Jump to go up a floor, or sneak to go down.

Good to know

  • Elevators can be up to 40 blocks apart.
  • There's a 1 second wait between uses.
  • Leave room to stand on the destination floor. Torches, signs, buttons, levers, vines and banners don't get in the way.
  • You'll see which floor you're on at the top of the screen.
