mcMMO

mcMMO lets you level skills like mining, woodcutting and fishing, unlocking abilities that make them easier.

Skills and abilities

/<skill>, like /mining, shows your level and abilities. /<skill> ? <page> shows more detail.

To use an ability, right-click with the skill's tool to ready it, then hit a matching block. For example, ready a pickaxe and break stone for Super Breaker. If you've got something in your offhand, crouch and right-click.

Some skills are passive only. Fishing shortens waits and improves catches, and Alchemy unlocks more potions and speeds up brewing.

Disabled for balance

  • Skills: Unarmed, Axes and Swords in PvP, and Salvage on gold.
  • Abilities: Second Smelt on gold ore, Blast Mining, and Double Drops on gold and diamond ore.
