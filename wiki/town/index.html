Town guide

A town is a group of players sharing protected land. These basics are enough to run a small one, and the rest you can pick up as you go.

The essentials

  • Join a town: ask for an invite, or join an open one with /t join <town>. If invited, type /accept or click the chat prompt.
  • Create a town: it costs 64g. Stand where you want it, run /t new <name> and accept. You start with one chunk.
  • Claim land: each chunk costs 16g. Deposit gold with /t deposit <amount>, stand in an unclaimed chunk and run /t claim. See Claiming land.
  • Join a nation: use /n join <name> or accept an invite with /t invite accept <nation>. The capital must be within 400 chunks (6,400 blocks) of your homeblock. The nation must be open or have invited you.

Tip: don't hand new residents a finished house and free gear. People stick around longer when they build up their own progress.

Town permissions

There are four types, all off by default:

  • Build: place blocks.
  • Destroy: break blocks.
  • Switch: use chests, furnaces, levers, hoppers and droppers.
  • Item: use water, lava, buckets, lighters, bone meal, ender pearls and bottles.

Mayors grant them through ranks or by selling plots. They can also be set for allies and outsiders, for example /t set perm ally build on.

Plots

A plot is a chunk inside a town, managed on its own. Only people with claim rights can add land to a town, but residents can buy plots in it. Press F3+G to see chunk borders.

  • /plot fs <price> puts the plot you're standing on up for sale.
  • /plot claim buys it, giving you full permissions there.
  • /plot unclaim gives it up.

Tip: giving plots away can be worth it. Residents leave if they've got nowhere to build.

Plot types

Set one with /plot set <type>. A plot has one type at a time.

TypeWhat it does
DefaultGeneral use, like homes and town bases
ShopRequired for shops
ArenaPvP on, and players keep their inventory on death
FarmNation residents can harvest crops but not break other blocks, and mobs can be hurt
EmbassyAnyone can buy it, from any town. Handy for overseas shops
InnNon-owners can use beds
WildsOres, logs, leaves, flowers and similar can be broken. Good for public mines
JailHolds jailed players
BankA bank plot for the town

Plot commands

Plot permissions override town permissions.

CommandWhat it does
/plot fs [price]Put the plot up for sale
/plot claimBuy an available plot
/plot unclaimRemove yourself from a plot
/plot set [type]Set the plot type
/plot set [minjoindays/maxjoindays] [number/clear]Reserve plots for new or long-time players
/plot set perm [build/destroy/switch/itemuse]Set plot permissions
/plot toggle [explosion/fire/mobs/pvp]Toggle plot behaviour

Town ranks

Assign them with /t rank add <player> <rank>.

RankWhat it can do
Co-mayorNearly everything a mayor can, including town and plot commands
AssistantClaim land, invite players, use plot commands, toggle public status and jail outlaws. Can't be kicked, and is first in line if the mayor is deleted
BuilderBuild, break, use items and switches on town-owned land
SheriffJail and unjail players

Inactivity

A player who doesn't log in for 40 days is deleted, losing their town and plots. If that's the mayor, an Assistant takes over.

Town perks

  • You regenerate health in your own or allied towns, and don't lose hunger there.
  • You keep your inventory when you die in your own town.
  • A resident can own up to 100 plots, and plots can cost up to 512g.
  • There's no daily upkeep or tax.

Ruined towns

A fallen town stays as a ruin for up to 24 hours. After 1 hour, one of its residents can bring it back with /t reclaim for 4g.

Merging towns

/t merge <town> lets a town absorb another, keeping claims, residents and plots. It costs 8g per absorbed chunk, both mayors must agree, and their homeblocks must be within 10 chunks.

Public spawn

/t toggle public lets others use your town spawn. Mayors can charge up to 10g per visit with /t set spawncost, paid to the town.

Renaming

Renaming a town costs 32g. Town names can be up to 20 characters, with no numbers.
