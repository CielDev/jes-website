Server rules

1. Cheating, exploits and alts

  • 1.1 Cheats: No cheat clients, macros or stolen accounts. Performance, HUD, minimap and cosmetic mods are fine, as is Litematica without a printer.
  • 1.2 Assisting: Knowingly helping or benefiting from a rule break counts as breaking it.
  • 1.3 Exploits: Using bugs without telling staff can mean a permanent ban.
  • 1.4 Alts: One account per player, no sharing. Deliberate alts get banned.
  • 1.5 Disruption: Causing lag on purpose or harming our services can mean a permanent ban.

2. Behavior and chat

  • 2.1 Naming: Nations must match a real current or past nation, region, province or large area, with the capital inside those borders. Town names can't be inappropriate, toxic or offensive, and a real place name goes to the town closest to that place. Usernames, nicknames, skins and titles can't be advertising, explicit or inappropriate.
  • 2.2 Raiding: Raiding and stealing are allowed. Protect your stuff.
  • 2.3 Spam: Don't repeat messages or disrupt chat.
  • 2.4 Behavior: No bullying, hate speech, encouraging self-harm or violence, sexual content or harmful false claims about people. Grooming or inappropriate relationships are strictly banned, on and off the server.
  • 2.5 Impersonation: Don't impersonate players, towns or nations.
  • 2.6 Personal information: Never share someone's personal information without explicit permission, or use it to threaten anyone.
  • 2.7 Links: No malicious, sexual or extremely violent links, or links that help break rules.
  • 2.8 Advertising: Only server-related advertising.
  • 2.9 Real-money trading: No selling in-game items or services for real money, or the reverse.
  • 2.10 Language: Keep global chat in English. Other languages go in private channels.

3. Griefing

  • 3.1 Near claims: Don't damage land in or within 5 chunks of a claim, and don't found towns near others to disrupt them. That's a warning and rollback first, and repeat offenses can mean a permanent ban.
  • 3.2 Wilderness: Changing unclaimed land away from claims is allowed, but anyone can ask for major damage to be rolled back. Don't break railways or ice roads linking two towns.
  • 3.3 Claims: No claim blocking. Towns can't have unclaimed chunks inside their borders, and overclaims can't split a town. Ships are exempt, and ocean outpost arms are fine if unclaimed later.
  • 3.4 Blocks: Taking valuable blocks is allowed, unless they're easy to get.
  • 3.5 Terraforming: Don't make changes visible on the map, like draining lakes or building islands. Vertical terraforming is fine.
  • 3.6 Map art: Allowed if it doesn't interfere with towns or involve major terraforming, and it must follow section 2. Greenland is recommended. Elsewhere, claim it.
