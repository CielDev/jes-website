Nation guide

Nations are groups of towns, and where the politics starts.

Create a nation

It costs 1,024g, paid from the town bank, and only a mayor can do it. Their town becomes the capital. Run /n new <name>, and make sure the name follows rule 2.1.

Nation basics

  • /n ally add <nation> adds an ally. There's no limit.
  • /n add <town> invites a town.
  • /n kick <town> removes a town.
  • /n toggle open controls public joining.
  • /n set spawn sets the spawn anywhere in the capital.
  • /n list ranks nations by citizens, so recruiting moves you up.

Nation range

A town's homeblock must be within 400 chunks (6,400 blocks) of the capital's homeblock to join.

Relocation

/n set capital <town> moves the capital for free. Renaming a nation costs 128g.

Nation ranks

RankWhat it can do
AssistantInvite towns, manage allies and enemies, and give the Helper rank
HelperAdd towns and deposit gold

Merging nations

/n merge moves all towns from one nation to another for free, and both kings must agree. The other nation is deleted, and towns out of range become nationless.
