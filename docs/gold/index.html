Gold

Gold ingots are the currency, and each ingot is one unit. Gold keeps its value because the game constantly removes it through costs like claiming land, so players supply most of what's for sale.

Store your gold

Your ender chest is the safest place. Gold can also go in a town or nation bank, and residents with the right rank can use it for expenses.

CommandWhat it does
/t deposit [amount]Add gold to the town bank
/n deposit [amount]Add gold to the nation bank
/t withdraw [amount]Take gold from the town bank
/n withdraw [amount]Take gold from the nation bank

How to get gold

Mining

Gold ore is spread evenly across the world, and equally common from Y0 down to Y-64.

Raiding

When a town falls, its protections go too. You can find abandoned towns and loot them, because raiding and stealing are allowed.

Voting

Run /vote and click the two links. Each vote gives one gold crate, up to two per 24 hours, and each crate can hold up to 64g or a special item. You must be online to receive rewards.

Shops

Sell items to other players. Stock, fair prices and a good location all matter, and nation capitals are the best spots because of the traffic at spawn. See Shops.
