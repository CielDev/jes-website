Every continent is built to scale, so you can find your own country, your city, or the coast you grew up on. Pick a place on the map and start building there.

Trade with a gold
ingot economy

Gold ingots are the server currency. Earn them by mining, collecting taxes, and selling goods in player shops. Prices are set by players, not by the server.
