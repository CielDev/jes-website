Expand your
Minecraft empire

Found a town with Towny, invite residents, and set their permissions. Sell plots to build a real estate market. Join towns together into a nation and watch it grow.

Explore Earth
at 1:300 scale

Every continent is built to scale, so you can find your own country, your city, or the coast you grew up on. Pick a place on the map and start building there.

Trade with a gold
ingot economy

Gold ingots are the server currency. Earn them by mining, collecting taxes, and selling goods in player shops. Prices are set by players, not by the server.

Grow your skills,
go to war

Level up with MCMMO as you mine, fight, and farm. When towns and nations clash, Siege War lets you attack and defend claims, so every border has to be earned.

Build something that lasts

JES is a 1:300 Earth where towns grow into nations and rivalries last for months. Pick a spot, bring a friend, and put your name on the map.
